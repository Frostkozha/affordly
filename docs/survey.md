# Grocery Budget Survey (6 questions)

## Intro (paste at the top of the survey)
We are researching how people in the US plan and pay for groceries. It takes about 2 minutes, is anonymous, and nothing is sold to you.

## Screening (one line, before Q1)
Are you 25-45 years old and do you live in the United States? (Yes / No - if No, end the survey)

## Q1. In a typical week, about how much does your household spend on groceries (food and drink from stores, not restaurants)?
Select one.
- Under $50
- $50 to $99
- $100 to $149
- $150 to $199
- $200 to $299
- $300 or more
- I am not sure

(Also ask once: "How many people does this feed? 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 or more".)

## Q2. In the last 4 weeks, how often was your grocery receipt higher than you expected before you went to the store?
Select one.
- Never
- Once
- 2 to 3 times
- Most weeks
- Every week
- I do not plan or estimate a total beforehand

## Q2b. (Show only if you chose anything except "Never" or "I do not plan...") When the receipt was higher than expected, by roughly how much, usually?
Select one.
- Less than $10
- $10 to $24
- $25 to $49
- $50 to $99
- $100 or more
- I am not sure

## Q3. In a typical week, how much food do you throw away because you bought a package larger than you needed (for example a bunch of herbs, a bag of rice, a carton of cream)?
Select one.
- None
- A small amount, less than $5 of food
- $5 to $14 of food
- $15 to $29 of food
- $30 or more of food
- I do not know
- I throw food away, but not because of package size

## Q4. Which of these have you used in the last 12 months to plan meals or groceries? Select all that apply. For each one you pick, say whether you paid for it.
Options (each with: Free only / Paid):
- A meal planning app (for example Mealime, Eat This Much, Plan to Eat, Samsung Food)
- A grocery store app or a grocery delivery app (for example Instacart, Walmart, Kroger)
- A budgeting app (for example YNAB, Monarch, Rocket Money)
- A spreadsheet or notes app that I set up myself
- Recipe websites or social media
- A paper list or nothing in particular
- Other (please write in): ________

## Q5. If a tool kept your weekly grocery bill inside a budget you set, and you could use it for free during a trial, what is the most you would pay per month for it, if anything?
Select one.
- Nothing, I would only use a free tool
- Up to $2.99 per month
- $3 to $4.99 per month
- $5 to $7.99 per month
- $8 to $9.99 per month
- $10 or more per month
- I do not know

## Q6. Which best describes how you shop for groceries most weeks?
Select one.
- I follow a written plan or list and mostly stick to it
- I write a list and often buy other things
- I decide what to buy once I am in the store
- Someone else in my household mostly shops
- I use delivery or pickup most of the time

## Notes for the person posting (not part of the survey)
- Wording is neutral: no question names a product benefit, a price, or "waste" as a problem before asking. Q3 and Q5 offer "none" and "nothing" as options. Every question offers "not sure" or an escape option.
- The brief asked for 6 questions on these topics: Q1 spend, Q2 receipt gap (with a follow-up Q2b on size), Q3 food thrown away from package size, Q4 apps used and paid for, Q5 willingness to pay, Q6 current planning habit for segmentation. If you must keep exactly 6 screens, show Q2b on the same page as Q2.
- Q5 is stated willingness to pay. People usually overstate it, so use the pattern across answers (not one price) and do not quote it as a conversion rate.
- Show answer order as written for Q1 to Q5; randomise only the Q4 option list.
- Do not offer money or a coupon for answers; it changes who responds. Do not collect email in the same form.
- Report the sample as a convenience sample, not representative of US adults.
